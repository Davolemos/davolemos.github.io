Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

choque studio, a strategic design agency in Panama City. For any question about your data, write to choquecreativo@gmail.com.

What data we receive

Only what you send us when you contact us on WhatsApp, by email, on Instagram or through the project form: your name, your contact details and what you tell us about your project. If you fill in the project form, we also receive what you write in it (name, brand, email or WhatsApp, budget and message).

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare proposals and carry out your project. We don’t sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as we work together and as long as the law requires. After that, we delete it.

Cookies

We don’t use cookies or advertising tools. We count visits anonymously (which pages are seen, where visitors come from and how many contact us), without cookies or personal data. Your browser only stores the language you choose.

Google button

The “Add as a preferred source” button is loaded by Google from its servers when you reach the end of the page. If you use it, Google saves your preference in your account under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time, under Panama’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law 81 of 2019).
